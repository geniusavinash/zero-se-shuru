Privacy

Browsing needs no account. The jobs list is public, and your filters live only in the address bar.

If you create an account

We store your email and password through Supabase Auth (the password is stored hashed). We also store what you choose to add: your name, college, graduation year, preferred roles and cities, and the jobs you save with their status and note.

Who can see it

Only you can read your profile and saved jobs: the database enforces it with row-level security. The site's admin sees only totals, such as the number of accounts and saves. The person who runs the site can open the database to keep the service working.

Where it is stored

In Supabase, in its Mumbai (India) region. The site itself is served by Cloudflare Pages, which keeps standard request logs.

What we do not do

No ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and no selling or sharing of your data. Your browser keeps your sign-in session in local storage; nothing else is stored on your device.

Deleting your account

Open an issue on GitHub asking for your account to be deleted. Do not put your email or any personal details in the issue: we will reply there with a private way to confirm the account is yours, then delete your account, profile and saved jobs.

Job listings

Listings come from public sources: companies' own career pages and the Adzuna API. To have a listing removed, use the same contact.
